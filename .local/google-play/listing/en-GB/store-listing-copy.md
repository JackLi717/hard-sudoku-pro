## App name

Platon Sudoku

## Short description

Logical Sudoku with step-by-step hints, five levels, and replayable moves.

## Full description

Play classic Sudoku with 10,000+ offline puzzles across five technique-based levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Extreme.

Get a step-by-step hint that shows the observation, logic and conclusion before you decide whether to apply it. Hints use supported solving techniques rather than guessing or backtracking.

Replay recorded moves, candidate edits, undos and hints from a completed game. Explore possible logical explanations for key moves, with unknowns kept clear.

Keep manual notes separate from an editable quick-candidate draft. Track completed games and statistics across all five levels.

No account required. Core play, hints and replay work offline, and progress and statistics stay on your device. No forced ads interrupt a puzzle. When available, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to refill one Smart Hint or Quick Candidates use.
