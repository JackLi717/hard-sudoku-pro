Bottle Harmony is an offline water sort puzzle game for Android, for players who enjoy planning moves, sorting colors and solving puzzles without a countdown or ads.

Start with color sorting

Learn the tap-and-pour rules in the opening puzzles, then progress through 1,000 mainline levels. Undo and restart are always free, and there is no countdown.

Settle into an offline puzzle

Play without an account or an internet connection. Choose from 15 glass styles, turn sounds off when you want quiet and keep your progress on this device.

Try a different challenge

Explore frozen-bottom side puzzles or 100 color memory puzzles. Optional color symbols offer another way to tell colors apart.

Currently in Google Play closed testing for players aged 13 and over, subject to local age ratings and regional availability. Android 7.0 or later and Google Play access are required. A few useful answers

Before you
start pouring.

Why can’t I install from the store?

The game is in closed testing. First join the Google group, then opt in to the test, using the same Google account at all three steps. Check the account selected in Google Play. If access is still unavailable, regional or device restrictions may apply; email us for help.

Is there an iPhone download?

This website currently offers the Google Play Android closed test. There is no public App Store download available here. Public release dates have not been announced.

How do the hints work?

Every new mainline or frozen-bottom bonus puzzle has one free successful one-step hint. Later hints cost one credit. First mainline clears earn credits, up to five stored credits. Completed-level replay hints and memory puzzle hints are free. Undo and restart are always free.

Will my progress be saved?

Progress and preferences are saved locally on your device. Normal launches and app updates retain them. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes them; cloud saving and cross-device restoration are not available.

Does offline play mean no analytics?

Gameplay works offline. Supported mobile builds enable Firebase usage analytics by default and can send gameplay events when connected. There is no in-app analytics opt-out switch. Please read the privacy policy before installing.

What feedback is useful?

Tell us what felt satisfying, confusing or too difficult. For a bug, include the device model, Android version, app version, puzzle number and steps to reproduce it. Avoid including account details, notifications or other personal information in screenshots.
