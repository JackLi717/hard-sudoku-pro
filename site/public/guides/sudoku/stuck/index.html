What to look for

Start with one digit

Not sure where to look? Try a digit that already appears often. Here we choose 8 and look for boxes still missing it.

Look inside this box

This box needs a 8. The marked digits rule out the crossed cells: a row or column cannot repeat 8.

Only one place remains

The box must contain a 8. After ruling out the other positions, only the highlighted cell remains.

Use the same method again

Look at another box missing 8. The existing 8s rule out positions until only one place remains here too.

Keep looking for your next step

Scan the boxes for the same digit. If you find no clear move, try another digit. Fill a cell when you can explain why.
