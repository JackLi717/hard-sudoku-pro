Hidden Pair · Step-by-step example

Within one row, column, or box, two digits can appear only in the same two cells. Keep those two digits in the paired cells and remove any other candidates from them.

Recognize the pattern

Within one row, column, or box, two digits can appear only in the same two cells.

How to find it

Scan a region one digit at a time. Find two digits whose possible cells are exactly the same pair.

What it allows

Keep those two digits in the paired cells and remove any other candidates from them.

Avoid the mix-up

The paired cells may have extra candidates before elimination. A naked pair starts with cells containing only the two digits.
