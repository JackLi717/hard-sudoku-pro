Single-Digit Grouped AIC · Step-by-step example

A grouped node means at least one of several candidates is true; it can participate in an alternating inference chain. Eliminate a target seen by the chain’s forcing endpoints.

Recognize the pattern

A grouped node means at least one of several candidates is true; it can participate in an alternating inference chain.

How to find it

Check that the grouped cells share the required region, then trace the strong and weak links through one or more group nodes.

What it allows

Eliminate a target seen by the chain’s forcing endpoints.

Avoid the mix-up

A true group does not mean every member is true. Preserve the group’s OR meaning when following the chain.
