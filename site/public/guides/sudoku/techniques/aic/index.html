Nice Loop / AIC · Step-by-step example

An alternating inference chain connects candidate states with verified strong and weak links. Open and closed chains have distinct conclusions. Use the verified outcome: remove a candidate or place a digit when its opposite state is impossible.

Recognize the pattern

An alternating inference chain connects candidate states with verified strong and weak links. Open and closed chains have distinct conclusions.

How to find it

Trace each implication from one end, recording strong and weak transitions and whether the chain closes or contradicts an endpoint.

What it allows

Use the verified outcome: remove a candidate or place a digit when its opposite state is impossible.

Avoid the mix-up

A strong link says: if one end is false, the other is true. A weak link says: if one end is true, the other is false. Do not reverse these implications; check the endpoint relation before concluding.
