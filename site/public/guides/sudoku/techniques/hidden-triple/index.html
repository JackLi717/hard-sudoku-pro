Hidden Triple · Step-by-step example

Within one row, column, or box, three digits are confined to the same three cells. Remove every other candidate from those three cells.

Recognize the pattern

Within one row, column, or box, three digits are confined to the same three cells.

How to find it

Scan three digits in one region and see whether all their possible positions fit within just three cells.

What it allows

Remove every other candidate from those three cells.

Avoid the mix-up

Each digit need not appear in all three cells. A naked triple is found by counting candidates inside three cells, not positions for three digits.

Step-by-step example · Row

This example starts from a saved intermediate position. Its candidates include earlier logical eliminations; they are not fresh automatic notes.
