Forcing Net · Step-by-step example

A forcing net branches and merges candidate implications. It can refute an assumption or prove a common result across alternatives. Remove a refuted candidate or apply the result shared by all exhaustive branches.

Recognize the pattern

A forcing net branches and merges candidate implications. It can refute an assumption or prove a common result across alternatives.

How to find it

Track each premise through the branching network, check every link, and compare contradictions or common endpoints.

What it allows

Remove a refuted candidate or apply the result shared by all exhaustive branches.

Avoid the mix-up

Do not skip a branch or confuse a shared effect with an effect from only one path.
