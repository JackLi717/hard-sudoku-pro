Locked Triple · Step-by-step example

Three cells in a row–box or column–box intersection contain only three digits between them. Remove those three digits from every other cell in the shared line and box.

Recognize the pattern

Three cells in a row–box or column–box intersection contain only three digits between them.

How to find it

Check the three cells where a row or column crosses a box. Look for a combined set of exactly three candidates.

What it allows

Remove those three digits from every other cell in the shared line and box.

Avoid the mix-up

The three cells need not have identical candidates. Every candidate in them must belong to the same three-digit set.
