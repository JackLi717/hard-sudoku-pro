Rows, columns and boxes

Fill the empty cells with 1–9. Every row, column and 3×3 box must contain each digit without repeats.

Enter a digit

Select an empty cell, then tap a digit. Given digits stay fixed; your entries appear in a different color.

Note the possibilities

When you are unsure, turn on Notes and tap digits. These small numbers are possibilities, not confirmed answers.

Find help when you need it

When you get stuck, tap Hint for help finding your next step.
