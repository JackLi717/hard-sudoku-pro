Free core play

Classic puzzles and editable auto notes remain free. Free players receive daily hint rewards. No ads start automatically when you begin, play or finish a puzzle.

Optional ads and Premium

When available, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for five extra Smart Hints. Lifetime Premium is an optional one-time purchase for unlimited Smart Hints and no ads, with no subscription.

Offline where it matters

Core play, puzzles, hints and replay work offline. Ads, purchases, purchase restoration and some privacy services may need an internet connection.

Before you play

A few useful answers.

Can I download Platon Sudoku now?

The Android app is currently in a Google Play closed test, not public release. If you are already an approved tester, open the Google Play testing page with the same Google account that was approved. The link does not grant testing access. If you are not a tester, please check this page again after the public launch.

Are all hints free and unlimited?

Free players have a hint balance and daily hint rewards. Optional rewarded ads can add hints when available. Unlimited Smart Hints are included with the optional Lifetime Premium purchase. Auto notes are free and unlimited for everyone.

Do I need an account or cloud saving?

No account is required. Game progress and statistics are stored on your device; the game does not provide account-based cloud saving. For optional analytics, diagnostics, ads and purchase details, read the Platon Sudoku privacy policy.

Where can I get help?

Email admin@readytradie.com with your question. If you are reporting a problem, your app version and a brief description of what happened help us understand it.
